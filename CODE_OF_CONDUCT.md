# Code of conduct

This project is for people making stories, lessons and shows with AI. Be someone others want to build with.

1. Be respectful and constructive. Critique work, never people.
2. No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks or unwelcome sexual attention, in any space connected with this project.
3. Keep other people's private information private.
4. Share only what you have the right to share: your own work, or work you may use.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who keep breaking them. Report problems privately to the maintainers through the Security tab's private reporting, or through [centerbeam.ai](https://centerbeam.ai).
